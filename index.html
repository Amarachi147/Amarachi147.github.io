About

I learn by building things that actually work.

I’m Owoh Amarachi Confidence, an AI automation specialist. I got here the practical way: pick a real problem, build the system, break it, fix it, repeat. WhatsApp assistants, voice agents, and workflows that quietly take repetitive tasks off a team’s plate.
